# Introduce yourself — who's here and why?

> Maintainer note: post in **👋 Show and tell** (or General) as a seed
> thread. Delete this note before posting.

The fixes for this project come from three overlapping crowds, and it
helps everyone to know who's in the room. If you're reading this in the
first weeks, say hi — two lines is plenty.

**Handy template:**

- **I'm a…** (job seeker / career coach / recruiter-curious / engineer who
  just likes parsers / etc.)
- **I'm here because…** (ghosted too many times / want a teaching tool /
  want to contribute / building something adjacent)
- **One thing filters did to me or someone I know…** (optional — and keep
  it synthetic/anonymized per the pinned rules!)

No real names, workplace names, or resume details — handles and roles are
plenty. And yes, "lurker planning to learn" is a completely valid
introduction; that's most of us at first.

I'll start in the first reply with the maintainer's version. 👋
