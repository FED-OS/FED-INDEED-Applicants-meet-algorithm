# 💬 Welcome to FED-Indeed Discussions

This community exists to fix an unfair information gap: companies know
exactly how their algorithms read your resume — you don't. FED-Indeed
closes that gap, and this is where we talk about it.

## Pick the right category

| Category | Use it for |
|---|---|
| 📣 **Announcements** | Releases, roadmap changes, calls for help (maintainers only) |
| 💬 **General** | Anything that doesn't fit elsewhere |
| 💡 **Ideas** | Proposals, design debates, roadmap feedback — the best ideas become [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) items |
| 🙏 **Q&A** | Usage questions; marked answered when resolved |
| 👋 **Show and tell** | Your before/after audits, deployment stories, "I landed the interview" wins |
| 😐 **Polls** | Community votes that reorder roadmap priorities |

## The rules, short form

1. **No real resumes or personal data.** Redact or synthethize. People
   come here precisely *because* they don't want their data spread around.
2. The [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies fully. Be kind;
   everyone here has been through the job-search wringer.
3. Search before posting — an answered Q&A beats a duplicate ten times out
   of ten.
4. **Security issues never go here.** Use the private advisory form in
   [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## How to ask a good Q&A question

Include what you expected vs. what happened, how you ran the app, your
Python/OS versions, and a *synthetic* description of the document (pages,
columns, creating tool). The `wiki/` folder — especially
[Troubleshooting](../wiki/Troubleshooting.md) — often answers it first.

## How to pitch a good Idea

Lead with the *problem* ("ATS engines compute per-skill tenure; we don't"),
then the solution, then constraints you checked (local-first, no new deps —
see [ADR.md](../ADR.md)). Ideas that gather support get promoted to roadmap
stages.

## Contributing code

Discussions are the front porch — the workshop is
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Start with
[`good first issue`](https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/FED-Indeed/labels/good%20first%20issue)
labels; the [starters](starters/) folder here lists warm-up threads too.
