## Maintainer Responsibilities

1. **Triage issues** weekly: label, route, close stale duplicates.
2. **Review PRs** within a few days — especially privacy and parsing
   correctness (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#review-process)).
3. **Guard the constitution**: reject changes that introduce persistence,
   telemetry, or non-optional network calls without an accepted ADR
   ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#decision-ladder)).
4. **Cut releases** per the checklist in [BUILD.md](BUILD.md#release-checklist)
   and keep [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) accurate.
